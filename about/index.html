About TripVibz

Ask someone who knows.

Review sites are mostly written by people who were in town for a weekend. TripVibz asks the people who know a city where they'd take a friend, what they'd skip and when to come.

How it works

  • Questions. Every city has a handful of questions people argue about, like "Navy Pier: worth it, or skip it?" One tap answers it. Missing the one you'd ask? Suggest it and we'll look at adding it.
  • Takes. Add a sentence to your answer and it becomes a take. Other people mark it True or Nope, so the best advice rises.
  • Where they'd take a friend to eat. Sign in and name your top 3 restaurants in a city, with a line on why. The city page adds everyone's picks together and puts verified locals' picks first.
  • The Guide. A map of each city showing the spots people's takes are about and the restaurants they picked, with how many people picked each one and what they said. Hotels are at the bottom. When you add a take, say where it is and it lands on the map.
  • Vibes. Tag a "Do this" take or a restaurant with who it's for or what kind of night it is, like Date night, With the kids or Loud and late. A vibe shows up once two people have given it. When people have tagged places in a city with the same vibe, it gets its own page, like Date night in Chicago.
  • Local badges. If you sign in, you can say how long you've lived in a city. The ✓ comes later, after we've seen you near the city on several days over several weeks. Until then your badge says "unverified". How we verify locals.
  • The month-by-month calendar. Festivals, conventions, weather and price seasons. We look them up from official sources and recheck them every month, and each entry links to where it came from.

Who's behind it

I'm James Overholt. I also publish Miami Take, a South Florida travel site. The stories people read most there were always the things a friend who lives in Miami would tell you, so I built TripVibz to ask that friend in every city.

TripVibz pays for itself with hotel links. If you book through one, the booking site pays us a commission.
